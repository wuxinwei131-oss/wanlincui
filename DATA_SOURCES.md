# Data sources and acknowledgments

The selected frozen inputs are identified by SHA-256 in ARCHIVE_MEMBERS.csv and DATA_RIGHTS.csv. Software terms are in LICENSE and author-owned data terms are in DATA_LICENSE.md. Public-domain observations and upstream content keep their original status. No raw national raster collections or complete third-party archives are redistributed here.

For a future Level B run obtain the actual registered NASS county-year labels, CDL crop masks, MOD09A1 optical records, gridMET meteorology and TIGER geography through those providers. Use the preserved date windows, domain exclusions, quantile/model settings and seeds in config/method_parameters.json. Earth Engine additionally requires the user's authorized account/project and service terms; no account/project is provided here. Re-downloading current services may differ from the original retained snapshots. Full raw-input checksums and exact historical runtime evidence would be needed before claiming an identical B-level run.

Dependencies are installed from their providers, not vendored. The checked distributions and license metadata are listed in DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.csv. PyMuPDF is absent. Package-level metadata may summarize multiple licenses (for example bundled numerical/geospatial libraries); this package does not relicense those dependencies. Author-supplied copyright notices and contributor credit are in LICENSE and AUTHORS.md.

The 33 retained core members are enumerated by hash in ARCHIVE_MEMBERS.csv and DATA_RIGHTS.csv. CC BY 4.0 applies only to author-owned rights as stated in DATA_LICENSE.md. Public-domain source observations, public identifiers and upstream products are excluded from that grant. No raw weather grids, CDL/MODIS raster collections or TIGER geometry are included.
