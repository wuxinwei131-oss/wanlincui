# Evidence and numerical precision

Level A starts from retained forecasts, observed county outcomes, actions, support and evaluation weights. It cannot certify an end-to-end rerun of original data services and model fitting. Outputs cover 2019–2024, with temporal corn ending in 2023. No 2025/2026 outcomes are included. FIPS are five-character strings; source_row_id and scientific ordering are retained. Fitting weights (mean-one within each assembled fitting set) are distinct from sum-one evaluation weights.

The original S9 Parquet records, ten-significant-digit evaluation CSVs, archived statistics and workbook displays are distinct evidence layers. Writing retained numbers with 17 round-trip digits does not add scientific precision. Six soybean Z1 endpoint comparisons retain max errors near 5e-10, exceeding the unchanged 1e-10 threshold. Original Z1 versus Z2 endpoint evidence was exactly equal in the retained original archive; no model was rerun. The original soybean Z2−Z1 archived PB3 point/range is −0.0016915956563888898 [−0.004585563092916671, 0.0008054658416666644]; CSV recomputation differs slightly. S9 workbook display values must not substitute for those inputs.

Actual historical-reference source calls HuberRegressor() without constructor arguments or sample weights. No contemporaneous fitted-estimator parameter snapshot was found in the verified archive. Explicit defaults inferred from a preserved environment are not proof of every original fit. Installing the verification dependencies below does not repair that historical evidence gap. Manuscript wording revisions remain subject to author approval.

The optional package figure outputs are newly rendered artifacts, not modifications to the approved manuscript.

Historical C0 preprocessing and C1L prefix-run variants are retained by the author for reviewer access under the confirmed code rights, after removal of private locators. These are not asserted to be byte-equivalent to the selected resumed source: observed differences include a crop-fraction mean implementation, ElasticNet precomputation, LightGBM thread settings and restart/output control. They are unnecessary for frozen-result Level A, but must be consulted before reproducing the exact historical execution sequence at Level B. Synthetic-only test evaluators and obsolete dispatch wrappers are excluded from the scientific runtime.
